Opening 0:00–0:10

Say:

"Welcome to Rainbow of Serenity, a meeting of Sex and Love Addicts Anonymous. My name is [name], and I'm a sex and/or love addict, and your chairperson for today."

Say:

"Let's open with a moment of silence, followed by the Serenity Prayer."

(pause, silence for about 10 seconds)

Higher Power, grant me the serenity to accept the things I cannot change, courage to change the things I can, and wisdom to know the difference.

Say:

"This is a closed meeting: it's for those who identify, or think they might identify, as a sex and/or love addict. This meeting is also a dedicated, affirming space for our LGBTQIA+ members. Allies in the fellowship are welcome too, as long as we all keep that focus at the center of the room."

Say:

"What is said here, who you see here, when you leave here, let it stay here. Anonymity is the spiritual foundation of all our traditions."

Say:

"This week is our [step study / Big Book reading / speaker / open + activity] meeting. Can I get a volunteer to read [the step / the tradition / the passage] for this week?"

(pause for a volunteer, then let them read)

Readings: Twelve Steps · Twelve Traditions · more literature.

Say:

"Let's go around. First name only, and 'sex and/or love addict' if that fits for you. I'll start: [name], sex and/or love addict."

(go around, one at a time)

If the group is large, trim this to name-only to protect sharing time.

Sharing 0:10–0:25

Speaker weeks

"This week we have a speaker. [Name], thank you for sharing your story with us. The floor is yours."

(when the speaker finishes, thank them, then open the floor below)

Say:

"With that, the floor is open. We've got about 15 minutes, so let's aim for around 2 minutes a share so more people get a turn."

The timer gently lets people know when they're near 2 minutes. After each share, a simple "Thank you, [name]" is enough.

If there's a silence, give it a moment. Then: "Is there anyone who hasn't shared yet who'd like to?"

On Open + Activity weeks, save a few minutes of this block for the group activity. The topic wheel is handy for picking a topic.

Closing 0:25–0:30

Say:

"That's time. Thank you to everyone who shared today. SLAA is fully self-supporting through our own contributions; we don't take outside funding. If you're able to chip in toward [Zoom costs / literature / group expenses], the link is in the chat and on our site."

Say:

"Any quick announcements? Service positions open, upcoming events?"

First Wednesday: remind everyone the business meeting starts right after we close, and it's open to all members.

Say:

"A reminder that what's shared in this room stays in this room. Take what you need and leave the rest."

Say:

"Let's close the way we opened, together: Higher Power, grant me the serenity to accept the things I cannot change, courage to change the things I can, and wisdom to know the difference."

(let the room finish together)

"Keep coming back. It works if you work it. See you next Wednesday."

Timed for a 30-minute Zoom meeting, to fit the free plan's limit for group calls. Adjust wording and timing to match how the meeting actually runs. Thank you for chairing.
