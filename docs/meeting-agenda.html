Rainbow of Serenity · SLAA

Meeting Agenda

Timed for a 30-minute Zoom meeting, in three blocks. Pairs with the opening and closing scripts.

0:00–0:10 Readings & Introductions

0:00–0:02Welcome, moment of silence, Serenity Prayer
0:02–0:03Closed / LGBTQIA+ affirming statement, anonymity reminder
0:03–0:06Reading — the week's format text, a Step, a Tradition, or Big Book passage
0:06–0:10Member introductions — first name and "sex and/or love addict," quick round

0:10–0:25 Sharing, open to all

0:10–0:25Open floor. On speaker weeks, the speaker goes first, then the floor opens. Aim for about 2 minutes a share so more people get a turn.

0:25–0:30 Closing

0:25–0:27Seventh Tradition & any announcements
0:27–0:28Closing reminder — what's shared here stays here
0:28–0:29Closing Serenity Prayer, together
0:29–0:30Send-off — "Keep coming back"
Built around the free Zoom plan's 30-minute limit for group calls. If the intro round runs long with a big group, trim it to name-only ("Hi, I'm [name]") rather than cutting into sharing time.
