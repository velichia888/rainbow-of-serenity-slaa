Chairperson's Closing Script

1. Time check & Seventh Tradition (2 min)

Say:

"That's time — thank you to everyone who shared today. SLAA is fully self-supporting through our own contributions; we don't take outside funding. If you're able to chip in toward [Zoom costs / literature / group expenses], the link is in the chat / on our site."

2. Announcements (1 min)

Say:

"Any quick announcements — service positions open, upcoming events?"

3. Reminder (1 min)

Say:

"A reminder that what's shared in this room stays in this room. Take what you need and leave the rest."

4. Closing prayer & send-off (1 min)

Say:

"Let's close the way we opened, together: Higher Power, grant me the serenity to accept the things I cannot change, courage to change the things I can, and wisdom to know the difference."

(pause — let the room finish together)

"Keep coming back — it works if you work it. See you next Wednesday."
