Is this meeting for me?

This is a closed SLAA meeting, which means it's for anyone who thinks they might be a sex and/or love addict. You don't need to be sure. If you're wondering, you're welcome. It's also an affirming space for LGBTQIA+ members, and allies are welcome too.

Not sure where you stand? SLAA's 40 Questions for Self-Diagnosis can help you think it through privately.

How to join on Zoom

  1. A few minutes before 3:00 PM Arizona time, tap Join Zoom above.
  2. On a phone, it will ask you to open or install the free Zoom app. On a computer, you can join in your web browser.
  3. If it asks, enter the meeting ID 858 4281 7235 and passcode 3eni7w.
  4. Pick the name people will see. A first name or an initial is perfect. You can change it anytime in Zoom under Participants → Rename.
  5. Camera on or off is completely up to you. Please stay muted unless you're sharing.

What happens in the 30 minutes

0:00Welcome, a moment of silence, and the Serenity Prayer.
0:03A short reading. It rotates each week between a Step, the Basic Text, a speaker, or an open topic.
0:06Quick introductions. Most people say their first name and "sex and/or love addict." Newcomers can just say "Hi, I'm [name], I'm new."
0:10Open sharing, about 2 minutes each. Use Zoom's Raise Hand (under Reactions, or More on a phone) if you'd like to share.
0:25Seventh Tradition, announcements, and the closing prayer together.

Good to know

  • You don't have to talk.Listening is a completely normal way to spend your first meetings. When it's your turn you can just say "I'll pass."
  • What's said here stays here.We don't repeat who we saw or what they shared, inside the meeting or outside it. That protects you too.
  • No sign-up, no fees.There's nothing to fill out and no dues. The Seventh Tradition is a voluntary contribution, and newcomers are never expected to give.
  • Keep coming back.Many people suggest trying about six meetings before deciding whether the program is for you.

Questions before your first meeting?

Email the group at rainbowofserenityslaa@gmail.com. It reaches the chair and secretary, not any one person's personal inbox.

Between meetings, the free Rainbow Companion app has a private recovery counter, daily readings, and the next meeting time.
