Privacy Policy

For the Rainbow of Serenity website and the Rainbow Companion app. Effective September 27, 2026.

The short version
  • Your recovery date, check-ins, gratitude list and step journal stay on your own device. We never see them.
  • You never need an account, a real name, an email or a phone number.
  • Only what you choose to post to the Community tab is shared, and everyone can see it.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics services, no cookies, and we never sell or share your information.

Who we are

Rainbow of Serenity is a volunteer-run SLAA (Sex and Love Addicts Anonymous) home group. This website and the Rainbow Companion app are run by the group, not by SLAA Fellowship-Wide Services. You can reach us at rainbowofserenityslaa@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

These features save only to your phone or browser's own storage. Nothing here is sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your recovery (clean) date and milestone celebrations
  • Daily check-ins and your gratitude list
  • Your step journal and which step you're working on
  • Settings such as light or dark mode and larger text
  • The name you last used in the Community tab, so you don't have to retype it

Images you make with "share as image" are created on your device and only go where you send them. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's site data, erases all of the above for good. We can't recover it, because we never had it.

What is shared: the Community tab

The Community tab is a shared feed that anyone with the app or website can read. When you post, we store:

  • The name you typed (we ask for a first name or initial only, and "Anonymous" is fine)
  • Your message (up to 500 characters; links aren't allowed)
  • The time you posted
  • A random anonymous ID for your device

That random ID comes from an invisible, anonymous sign-in (Firebase Anonymous Authentication). It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple ID. We use it only to limit each device to one post every 30 seconds, which keeps out spam.

Please keep what you share within our traditions of anonymity: no last names, and nothing that could identify you or anyone else.

Photo gallery

Members can send photos to the group's gallery, with an optional caption. Before a photo leaves your device, the app shrinks it and saves it as a fresh copy, which removes hidden details such as location, camera and date (EXIF data). Each photo waits for a moderator to approve it, and only moderators can see it until then. Approved photos can be seen by everyone. Please don't send photos of faces or anything that identifies a person or place.

Anonymous visitor count

The first time a device opens the website or app each day, we add 1 to that day's total. That number is the only thing sent. No ID, name, location or device details are stored with it. Your device remembers only the date it was last counted, so it isn't counted twice. Only moderators can see the totals.

Moderators

A small number of group volunteers can remove posts and approve or remove photos. They sign in with Google, and only their own Google account is used for that. Regular members never sign in with Google, and moderators can't see who wrote a post beyond the name the person typed.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) stores Community posts, gallery photos and the daily visitor count. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces used on the pages.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Zoom hosts our meetings. Opening the Zoom link takes you to Zoom, and its own privacy policy applies there.

Like any website, these services may see your device's IP address and basic browser details when they deliver content. We don't collect, keep or look at that information ourselves.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no advertising or tracking networks
  • No analytics services and no cookies
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera roll (beyond a photo you pick yourself), microphone or health data

Removing something you posted

Posts and photos can't be edited once shared. To have one removed, email rainbowofserenityslaa@gmail.com with roughly what it said and when you posted it, and a moderator will delete it. Moderators also remove anything that breaks our traditions or looks like spam.

Children

This website and app are meant for adults in SLAA recovery and are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change how the website or app handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top.

Questions about privacy? Email rainbowofserenityslaa@gmail.com.
