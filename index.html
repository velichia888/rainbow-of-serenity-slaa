Who This Is For

A closed, LGBTQIA+ affirming meeting

This is a closed SLAA meeting: it's for people who identify, or think they might identify, as a sex and/or love addict. You don't need certainty to attend — if you're questioning whether this program is for you, you're welcome here.

This meeting grew out of wanting a room where queer and trans members don't have to explain or translate their experience before they can talk about their recovery — where chosen family, coming out, and relationships of every shape are just part of the conversation, not something to footnote.

Allies in the fellowship are genuinely welcome too, as long as everyone shows up ready to keep that the room's center of gravity. What's shared here stays here — anonymity protects everyone, inside the meeting and outside it.

Recovery has no closet.

What to Expect

Our format rotates every week

One chairperson keeps the meeting running week to week, and every other role is filled by whoever volunteers that day. Showing up early to take a role is a good way to get more out of the meeting.

  • Greeter — welcomes newcomers and helps them find the link
  • Reader — reads the opening, format, or literature for the week
  • Timer — keeps shares within the time limit, gently
  • Tech host — runs the waiting room, screen share, and mute issues
Monthly rotation
WeekFormatWhat happens
Week 1 Step study We work through the Twelve Steps together, one at a time, with sharing tied to that step.
Week 2 Big Book reading We read aloud from SLAA's Basic Text and share on whatever the reading brings up.
Week 3 Speaker One member shares their story start to finish, then the floor opens for sharing.
Last week Open + activity Open sharing on any topic, plus a fun group activity or team-bonding time — recovery needs connection too.

Months with a fifth week: [chair's call — usually repeats speaker or open format].
