Rainbow of Serenity Companion — Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026. Rainbow of Serenity is an independent, community-focused recovery companion for adults. It is not an official product of SLAA Fellowship-Wide Services and is not a clinical or crisis service.

Information kept on your device

Your recovery date, milestones, daily check-ins, gratitude entries, personal recovery plan, settings, and selected Community display name are saved using local browser/app storage. The recovery records and plan are not uploaded to our Community database. This information is not encrypted by a separate Rainbow feature, backed up by Rainbow, or synchronized with another device. Other people with access to your unlocked device might see it. Deleting the app or clearing app storage may permanently erase it.

Community posts and your choice to share

Posting to Community is optional. New submissions remain visible only to moderators until approved. Approved Community posts are readable by other members and website visitors. When you post, Google Firebase stores your chosen display name, your message, the time, and an anonymous Firebase account identifier used for basic abuse and rate controls. Please do not include identifying personal details, including another person's information. Never post your private recovery plan or check-ins in the Community feed.

Firebase Authentication may create an anonymous account identifier without asking you to register. This does not make posted content private. Moderators may remove posts that violate community guidelines. Community posts may remain available until moderators remove them; there is no automatic deletion period guaranteed for submitted posts.

Reporting and blocking Community content

If you report a post, Firebase stores the reported post's ID, a reporting account identifier, the reason selected, the report time, and the review status. Reports are only readable by authorized moderators. Other members cannot see them. Blocking an account saves its anonymous account ID on your device to stop displaying that account's posts locally. Blocking is device-specific and does not prevent someone from creating a new anonymous account.

For removal requests, moderation concerns, or questions about how long shared material remains visible, contact rainbowofserenityslaa@gmail.com. Do not send private recovery journal content unless you choose to do so.

Third-party services

Google Firebase provides authentication and Community data storage; its policies are available at Firebase Privacy and Security. Google Fonts may load display fonts over the network. The recovery group's public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Meeting links may open Zoom, Calendar, a web browser or your email app; those services process information under their own privacy policies. Network services can receive basic connection data such as IP address and technical request details.

Your choices

You can use the local recovery features without writing a Community post. You can delete your recovery plan and individual local entries in the app, clear local app data, or request removal of Community content by contacting the moderators. The app does not offer an account-backed recovery of local journal entries. No advertising or cross-app profiling is implemented in the Rainbow code reviewed for this policy.

Contact and updates

This policy describes the Rainbow companion's current data practices. If they change, this page and its effective date will be updated. For privacy questions, contact rainbowofserenityslaa@gmail.com.

Need immediate help? This community app is not an emergency or medical service. Contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis-support service.
